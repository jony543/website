Should Compassion Cease to Beat Within Me is a bilingual humanist cabaret created in collaboration with [Luai Haj](https://www.facebook.com/loui.haj).

The play that weaves together music, storytelling, and poetry. The performance was created for Duet, an adult-oriented festival at Al-Mina Theater, through a unique model of collaborative creation between Arab and Jewish artists in Israel.

The play tells the story of an Arab young man born into a national and personal wound, who befriends a classmate from a military boarding school, only to lose him in the war. He is left with an impossible and deeply unsettling question: How do you mourn a dear friend while, in the very same breath, grieving for the members of your own people? Out of this contradiction emerges a choice—not to give up on compassion.

The different narratives within the protagonist’s soul are expressed through theatre accompanied by live music, interwoven with songs that move between contemporary styles and the aesthetics of classical Arab music.
